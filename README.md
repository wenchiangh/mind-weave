# MindWeave

Weave your knowledge into agent context.

MindWeave is a local-first knowledge layer for AI agents.

Modern AI tools are excellent at reasoning, but they often lack access to the knowledge that matters most: your notes, codebases, documents, workflows, and project context.

Today, this knowledge is scattered across multiple places:

- Markdown notes
- Obsidian vaults
- Code repositories
- Design documents
- Project workflows
- Personal knowledge bases

While each source may already have its own search or organization system, AI agents typically cannot access them in a unified, efficient, and continuously updated way.

MindWeave bridges this gap.

It continuously discovers and indexes knowledge from multiple local sources, makes it accessible as agent-friendly context, and exposes a unified query interface that can be consumed by any agent, tool, or workflow.

MindWeave does not aim to replace your note-taking application, code editor, documentation platform, or AI assistant.

Instead, it focuses on a single responsibility:

**Turning your distributed knowledge into context that agents can understand and use.**

## Principles

### Local First

Your knowledge stays under your control.

MindWeave is designed to work with local files, local repositories, and self-hosted environments without requiring cloud synchronization.

### Agent Agnostic

MindWeave does not belong to a specific AI platform.

Whether you use Claude Code, Codex, Gemini, Cursor, or future tools, your knowledge should remain accessible through a unified layer.

### Multiple Sources, One Knowledge Layer

Knowledge should not be locked into a single application.

MindWeave brings together notes, code, documents, and workflows into a unified searchable layer while preserving their original sources.

## Non-Goals

MindWeave is not:

- A chat application
- An AI assistant
- A note-taking application
- A document editor
- A second-brain platform

These are solved problems with many excellent tools already available.

MindWeave focuses on being the knowledge layer between your knowledge sources and your AI agents.

## Vision

A future where every agent can access the knowledge that matters, without requiring users to duplicate, migrate, or manually maintain it.

Your knowledge.

Your sources.

Your workflow.

Woven into agent context.
